# Hardware run V0: rekordbox stick with our export.pdb

**Date:** 2026-11-19

**Image V0:** a fresh rekordbox export of a 6-track playlist (local fixture `rekordbox-hw1`).
Only `PIONEER/rekordbox/export.pdb` is replaced, by
`cargo run --example rewrite_pdb` (commit d9917e0). Audio, ANLZ files, `exportExt.pdb`,
`exportLibrary.db` and the settings files are rekordbox's, unchanged.

- **Our export.pdb:** 40 pages, 163,840 bytes, sha256
  `2e0d2e5e40844d154502c9d5d24eb0e24df1ce9a7e3a5cdf6169086d14c26679`.
- **rekordbox's export.pdb:** 51 pages, sha256
  `5124545a264c072e3a91653aff000b9f15e220da23c128746cad4740d442f895`.
- **What differs** (ADR-012, ADR-017):
  - page layout and packing;
  - sequence numbers;
  - whole-page transaction fields;
  - `empty_candidate` placement;
  - history table 19 written empty.

  Track rows are re-encoded from the loaded database, so they keep rekordbox's `unknown2`
  and string slots.
- **Stick:** `CDJ_RB_HW1`, small and slow.

## Results

| Player | Result |
|---|---|
| CDJ-2000NXS | Works, as reported by the user: the stick loads and plays. |
| CDJ-2000 | Not tested yet. |

**Glitch:** the NXS once dropped the large waveform. The user has seen the same with
rekordbox-written sticks and attributes it to the slow stick. V0 doesn't touch that
waveform: it comes from the `.EXT` file (PWV3), which is rekordbox's here.

**Not yet confirmed individually:**
- browse menus;
- playlist order;
- BPM, grid and cues;
- non-Latin titles.

## What the NXS wrote back

The stick was copied back after the NXS session, without `Artwork/` and `extracted/`.
The copy is the local fixture `hw-runs/v0-nxs`.

- **export.pdb:** still 40 pages. The NXS changed only these pages:
  - page 0, the header (6 bytes);
  - pages 23 and 24, the index page and first data page of table 11 (history playlists);
  - pages 25 and 26, the same pages of table 12 (history entries).

  We wrote both tables empty, and the NXS filled them in place. The track, playlist and
  fixed-table pages are unchanged. Our reader still loads the file: 6 tracks, 1 playlist,
  6 entries.
- **exportExt.pdb:** one byte changed, on page 0.
- **New files:**
  - `LOG/<id>-alog_1.json` (176 bytes);
  - empty `CDJ/` and `MPJ/` directories.

## What this settles
- **Page layout:** the NXS accepts our page layout, sequences, transaction fields and
  `empty_candidate`s, and an empty table 19.
- **Biggest risk:** the risk that the player ignores our export.pdb is cleared for the NXS.
- **History tables:** the NXS records play history into the empty history tables we write
  (ADR-017).
